FAQ

Before you ask

How fast is the turnaround?

Short-form edits are usually ready within 1–2 days; long-form videos take a few days depending on length and complexity. I always confirm a delivery date before I start — and I hit it.

What do you need from me to start?

Just your raw footage and a short brief — what the video is about, any style references, and must-keep moments. Share files via Google Drive, Dropbox or Frame.io, whichever you prefer.

Can I see a sample before committing?

Yes. Message me and I'll cut a short trial edit from your own footage, so you can judge my style on your content before we go further.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes — feedback rounds are part of the process. You review the first cut, I refine it, and we keep going until it feels right.

What software do you edit in?

DaVinci Resolve is my primary editor (editing + color grading), with Premiere Pro, After Effects, Photoshop and Canva Pro in the toolkit for motion graphics, thumbnails and branding.

Do you work with clients worldwide?

Yes — I'm based in Sahiwal, Pakistan and work fully remotely with creators and brands in the USA, UK, Canada, Australia and beyond, in English, across time zones.
